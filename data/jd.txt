Senior Data Engineer

We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to design, build, and maintain scalable data infrastructure. You will work closely with analytics, ML engineering, and product teams to deliver high-quality data solutions.

Requirements:
- 5+ years in data engineering or related field
- Expert-level SQL and Python
- Deep experience with Azure cloud services: Data Factory, Synapse Analytics, Databricks, Fabric
- Strong dbt experience for data transformations and modeling
- Experience with Dagster or Airflow for orchestration
- Proficiency with DuckDB, PostgreSQL, and data lake architectures
- Experience with Medallion architecture (Bronze/Silver/Gold)
- Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Bicep, or Pulumi)
- CI/CD for data pipelines (GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps)
- Strong communication and collaboration skills
- Experience mentoring junior engineers

Nice to Have:
- Experience with Apache Spark optimization
- Knowledge of data mesh principles
- Familiarity with Delta Lake and Lakehouse architecture
- Experience with Power BI or semantic modeling
- Container orchestration (Kubernetes)
- Streaming data experience (Kafka, Event Hubs)
